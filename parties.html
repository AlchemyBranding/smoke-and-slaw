How it works

Same food.
Any occasion.

The setup is simple. We bring the food, we run the counter, your guests eat well. Whether it's a garden party or a warehouse, the approach is the same.

Everything is taken care of before we arrive

All the food is smoked overnight, transported to your event, and served from our counter. You focus on the party, we handle the rest.

Counter service keeps it social

Guests come to the counter, our team serves them, and they eat when they like. No waiting for a table to be called.

Works best at scale

We work best with a decent crowd. Drop us a message if you're not sure whether your event is a good fit.
