How it works

Street food energy.
Any occasion.

Slow cooked the day before, served at its best

All the food is prepared the day before your event and brought ready to serve. No grilling on-site, no smoke, no setup risk. The only thing we do on-site is toast the buns.

Guests come to the counter

No waiting for a plate to arrive. The queue is part of the vibe - people chat, pile their trays, and get stuck in. Everyone eats at roughly the same time.

Our team runs the service

We set up, serve, keep the counter stocked, and clear down at the end. You don't need to manage a thing.

We recommend a minimum of around 40 guests. Below that, the logistics don't work well for either side. If you've got fewer, drop us a line and we'll see if we can help.

We need a covered area to serve from - a gazebo, marquee, or indoor space. We handle setup and breakdown. No ventilation requirements - we're not smoking on-site. We'll confirm exact requirements once we know your venue.
