The food

Smoked overnight.

Everything is smoked low and slow overnight. It arrives ready, and it shows.

Signature

Beef Brisket

16-hour smoked Welsh beef brisket. No sauce needed. Also served as burnt ends, brisket point cubed, sauced, and smoked a second time in a toasted brioche bun.

Classic

Pulled Pork

20-hour hickory smoked pulled pork. Served with apple slaw, potato salad, and house pickles. Always the first to run out.

House favourite

Smokehouse Sausage

Rawling's Butchers' smokehouse sausage, hickory smoked. Served with apple slaw, potato salad, and house pickles.
