The food

Cooked the day before.
Served at its best.

Everything is prepared properly the day before your event - not rushed, not cut short. It arrives ready, and it shows.

Signature

Texas Smoked Beef Brisket

Juicy. Smokey. Gone. 14-hour Mesquite smoked Texas-style Welsh Beef Brisket. No sauce needed - it speaks for itself.

Fan favourite

Brisket Burnt Ends

The best bite in BBQ. Brisket point cubed, sauced, and smoked a second time until caramelised and falling apart. Served in a toasted Brioche Bun with Apple-Slaw, BBQ mayo, pickles and hot sauce.

House favourite

Smoke House Sausage

Rawling's Butchers' famous Smoke House Sausage, gently smoked over Hickory wood. Served with Apple-Slaw, Potato Salad and House Pickles.

All mains served with homemade Apple-Slaw, Potato Salad and House Pickles.

The process

Three steps.
That's it.

1

Enquire

Tell us your date, your guest count, and what you're after. We'll come back with availability and a clear quote - no fluff.

2

Plan your menu

Pick your mains and confirm your numbers. We handle everything else - the food is slow cooked the day before, then transported and served at your event.

3

We serve at your event

Our team arrives, sets up, and works the counter. Guests queue, we load their trays, and everyone eats well. Done.
